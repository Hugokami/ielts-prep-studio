### Audit Methodology
Every learning asset in IELTS Prep Studio was independently audited against the following criteria:
1. **Zero Reproduction of Real Cambridge Test Papers**: No passages, questions, answer keys, or audio files are copied or transcribed from the official Cambridge IELTS Book series (Books 1–19) or past actual exam papers.
2. **Original Thematic Drafting**: Passages are original educational compositions authored to mirror the linguistic register, lexical density (C1/C2 vocabulary), syntactic complexity, and factual tone expected in IELTS Academic Reading.
3. **Question Formulation**: All questions (True/False/Not Given, Matching Information, Summary Completion, Multiple Choice) and answer explanations were originally authored specifically for this application.

## 3. Academic Writing Models (40 Model Essays)

The writing suite comprises 40 analytical model responses across Academic Task 1 and Task 2:
* **Task 1 Models (15 Reports)**: Original synthetic visual prompts covering dynamic line graphs, comparative bar charts, proportional pie charts, data tables, natural process cycles, manufacturing assembly flows, and dual-era municipal redevelopment plans.
* **Task 2 Models (25 Essays)**: Discursive model essays addressing contemporary global topics (automation, urban planning, environmental economics, pedagogical technology, public health ethics).
* **Assessment Framework**: All feedback and scoring criteria are referenced against public, freely accessible Cambridge band descriptor descriptors (Task Achievement, Coherence & Cohesion, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range & Accuracy).
* **Origin**: Original compositions authored for IELTS Prep Studio. Zero prompts or student sample essays copied from past exam publications.

## 4. Speaking Practice Hub & Seasonal Topics

* **30 Practice Topic Sets**: Divided into Part 1 (introductory familiar scenarios), Part 2 (structured cue cards), and Part 3 (abstract thematic discussion prompts).
* **5 Response Frameworks**: Educational structuring methodologies (PREP: Point-Reason-Example-Point, STAR, PEEP, Cause-Effect, Past-Present-Future temporal contrast).
* **Origin**: Original conceptual prompts reflecting common everyday and academic conversation themes. Described strictly as *"practice topics"* without claims of proprietary knowledge of upcoming examination rotations.
